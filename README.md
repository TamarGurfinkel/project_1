# 📊 Firearm Availability & Violence in the U.S. — Data Analysis Project

Welcome to our data analysis project, completed as part of the "Introduction to Data Analysis" course at Ariel University.

In this project, we examine the relationship between firearm availability and lethal violence, including homicide and suicide rates, using historical data from the U.S.

---

## 📁 Project Description

This project is based on data from the years 1949–2020, taken from the paper:
**"State-level household gun ownership proxy dataset, 1949–2020"** (Kang & Rasich, 2023)

---

## ❓ Research Questions

1. Is there a relationship between firearm availability (measured by the FSS index) and overall homicide rates in the U.S. over time?
2. Does firearm availability affect suicide rates across different geographic regions over time?
3. Are there geographic differences between U.S. regions in terms of homicide rates and personal safety levels?

---

## 📌 Key Findings

- A significant positive correlation was found between firearm availability and homicide rates across U.S. states
- States with higher firearm availability consistently showed higher suicide rates
- Clear geographic disparities were found — states like Mississippi and Louisiana showed consistently high homicide rates, while Maine and Vermont showed consistently low rates
- A forecast for 2025–2030 suggests these geographic gaps are expected to continue
- The data shows that firearm availability is strongly associated with higher levels of violence in American society
